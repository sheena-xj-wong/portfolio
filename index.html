Podcasts · Partner Launch

Ximalaya 喜马拉雅 — Podcasts: Partner Launch

Why

Ximalaya, China's leading online audio platform, was joining Apple Podcasts, but their design team had no prior exposure to Apple's asset specifications, risking high rejection rates ahead of the September 2024 launch.

What / How

As sole Partner Asset Manager with Mandarin proficiency, I developed and delivered tailored Partner Art training for Ximalaya's designers, then project managed asset delivery across Business, Editorial, Design and Partner stakeholders to hit delivery milestones. The training became a reusable template, used to onboard internal Editorial and Design teams and repeatedly redelivered to Ximalaya given their high designer turnover.

Impact

Reduced asset rejection rates from a peak of 73% early in the review cycle to near 0% in the cycles immediately preceding launch, following delivery of tailored training and hands-on guidance, supporting an on-time launch. Processed 291 assets across Show, Channel and Subscription pages, and reviewed 35 Channel Pages and 128 Show Pages to confirm accurate display at launch.

Apple TV · Platform Redesign

Apple TV — Platform Redesign: Brand Page Audit

Why

Following a platform redesign, an engineering automation gap left 112 International Channel and Federated partners without updated artwork across several key promotional placements, risking an inconsistent experience for Apple TV users worldwide.

What / How

I led the team's response, launching a large-scale outreach project across all 112 affected partners. I proactively gathered title lists from partners to identify the full scope of missing artwork, then used an internal asset-building tool to create 924 new assets at scale, closing the automation gap without an existing playbook to follow.

Impact

Restored a consistent, up-to-date experience for Apple TV users worldwide across all 112 partners. Delivered 924 new assets spanning multiple placement types, turning a reactive engineering gap into a structured, repeatable remediation process.

Slack Bot & Workflow Automation

BA Art Reviewer

Why

Currently, escalating Podcasts artwork reviews to the BA team requires manually gathering metadata, translating descriptions, and tagging reviewers. Inconsistent formatting also forces the BA team to spend extra time hunting for context before they can respond.

What / How

A PAM DRI simply submits a quick form via the BA Art Reviewer Slack bot, and the bot automatically fetches metadata, translates descriptions, tags reviewers, and posts a standardized request for quick review.

Impact (Projected)

Significantly reduced manual overhead for both the PAM team and Business Affairs. By automating data collection and standardizing requests, this tool will eliminate time spent on context gathering, accelerate turnaround times for artwork approvals, and streamline the entire escalation workflow.

Automated Agent

Apple Originals: Programming Reminders

Why

Montage artwork update requests for the Home and Apple TV tab on Apple TV were submitted monthly by Editors, each tagged with a programming date, but nothing tracked them. The team has kept a zero-missed streak since August 2024 through manual Quip checks alone, but that relied on consistent manual effort and had no safety net if something slipped.

What / How

Designed a task scanner that checks Quip for requests against their tagged programming dates and sends automated weekly summaries, flagging anything coming up before it's missed.

Impact

Removes reliance on manual checks to protect the streak, giving the team a repeatable safeguard to keep it going rather than depending on catching everything by hand.
