Apple Podcasts Partner Enablement

Ximalaya 喜马拉雅 Launch

Why

Ximalaya, China's largest online audio platform, was joining Apple Podcasts but their design team had no prior exposure to Apple's asset specifications, risking high rejection rates ahead of the September 2024 launch.

What / How

As sole Partner Asset Manager with Mandarin proficiency, I developed and delivered tailored Partner Art training for Ximalaya's designers, then project managed asset delivery across Business, Editorial, Design and Partner stakeholders to hit delivery milestones. The training became a reusable template, used to onboard internal Editorial and Design teams, and has continued to support Ximalaya's design team through multiple redeliveries.

Impact

Reduced asset rejection rates from a peak of 73% early in the review cycle to near 0% in the cycles immediately preceding launch, following delivery of tailored training and hands-on guidance, supporting an on-time launch. The partnership has continued since, with Ximalaya's catalogue now surpassing 10 million episodes on Apple Podcasts.

Apple TV Cross-Platform Delivery

Brand Page Audit

Why

Following a platform redesign, an engineering automation gap left 112 international partners without multi-title promotional artwork in the latest format across several key offer placements, risking an inconsistent experience.

What / How

I led the team's response, launching a large-scale outreach project to gather title lists from affected partners and scope the missing artwork. We then used an internal asset-building tool to create and programme new assets using those lists, closing the gap without an existing playbook.

Impact

Restored a consistent, up-to-date experience for Apple TV users across macOS, iPadOS and iOS, delivering 924 new assets at scale.

Apple Podcasts AI-Assisted Tooling Workflow Improvement

BA Art Reviewer

Why

Escalating Podcasts artwork reviews to Apple's Business Affairs (BA) team currently requires manually gathering metadata, translating descriptions and tagging reviewers. Inconsistent formatting also forces the BA team to spend extra time hunting for context before they can respond.

What / How

A member of my team submits a quick form via the BA Art Reviewer Slack bot, which automatically fetches metadata, translates descriptions, tags reviewers and posts a standardised request for quick review.

Impact (Projected)

Currently pending security approval ahead of production rollout. Once live, this tool will significantly reduce manual overhead for both the PAM team and Business Affairs; by automating data collection and standardising requests, it will eliminate time spent on context gathering, accelerate turnaround times for artwork approvals and streamline the entire escalation workflow.

Apple TV Process Automation Agent Design

Apple Originals: Programming Reminders

Why

Editors submit monthly artwork update requests for Apple Originals on the Home and Apple TV tabs, each tagged with a programming date, but nothing automated tracked them. The team's zero-missed streak since August 2024 relied entirely on manual Quip checks, with no safety net if something slipped.

What / How

I designed an agent that checks Quip requests against their tagged dates and sends weekly summaries, flagging anything due the following week. It runs autonomously on Apple's internal backend so it keeps working even when I'm offline or out of the office.

Impact

Replaced manual vigilance with a repeatable safeguard, protecting the streak without relying on catching everything by hand.

Apple TV AI-Assisted Tooling Data Validation

TV Title Launch Checker

Why

Multi-title promotional artwork for Apple Originals relies on title lists submitted by international Editors. Manually validating and cross-checking these against release priorities in Quip was slow and prone to missing or duplicate title entries. This was especially critical before Engineering shipped a fix for a backend timing limitation, when titles launching after a refresh date risked having outdated artwork on an important launch day.

What / How

I built a web-based checker that reconciles submitted Airtable title lists against release priorities on Quip, flagging any titles launching after a set refresh date. Editors then have the chance to either move their refresh date or, at minimum, know in advance that some titles won't have the latest artwork at refresh.

Impact

Eliminated manual cross-referencing and continues to catch missing or duplicate titles before submission, helping maintain accurate, up-to-date promotional artwork.
